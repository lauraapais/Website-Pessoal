Vale de Cambra Arts and Performing Center (CAE) is a space dedicated to artistic and educational creation, presentation, and exchange, deeply rooted in the community. Located in the refurbished building of the city’s former cinema, it is embedded in the urban and cultural fabric of Vale de Cambra, working in close connection with other cultural facilities and local agents.

The center’s visual and communication materials was carefully designed to reflect its spirit — vibrant, contemporary, and community-oriented. The identity is dynamic and colorful, adapting to different contexts while maintaining a strong and recognizable presence. To amplify its reach, a dedicated website was also developed, serving as a platform to share the cultural program and strengthen the dialogue between the center and its audience.
