Work / CAE Vale de Cambra

CAE Vale de Cambra

@united by

(Graphic Design; Product Design; Full‑Stack Development)

2022—2026

The visual identity for CAE — Centro de Artes e Espetáculos emerges from the building itself and its relationship with the city. The architectural façade, defined by a modular grid of vertical and horizontal alignments, volumes, shadows, and reflections, became the starting point for the entire graphic system.

This structural logic was translated into a flexible visual language based on modular compositions and geometric rigor. The identity reflects the idea of a cultural center as a mutable, diverse, and constantly evolving space capable of hosting different artistic expressions while maintaining a strong and coherent presence.

Rather than relying on fixed colors or rigid layouts, the system embraces adaptability. Color, imagery, and pattern respond to each context, allowing the identity to shift in tone and atmosphere according to the cultural experience being communicated. This elasticity ensures clarity, recognizability, and consistency across all applications, from institutional communication to event promotion.
