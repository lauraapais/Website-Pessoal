TSE — Técnicos ao Serviço das Empresas

Freelance

(Graphic Design)

2025

The visual identity for the Technical Service for Enterprises (TSE) was developed to modernize the brand while retaining its core identity.

Building upon the company's established blue, a stronger, more vibrant shade was introduced. The acronym was constructed in a modular format, creating a dynamic and cohesive symbol. This approach gives the company a refreshed, less corporate look. The new identity was applied across a comprehensive range of materials including business cards, signage, and various corporate graphic materials.
