Disruptive
Academic Project

Editorial design
Graphic design
Exhibition

Disruptive is the planification of a fictional exhibition that addresses the work of Wolfgang Weingart. It is the first edition of a series of design exhibitions at Colégio das Artes. This exhibitions has the purpose of promoting the discipline and revealing part of the history of graphic design.

Throughout his career, Wolfgang Weingart has become an internationally known designer and typographer that helped change the area of graphic design by influencing others to break apart the norms and to experiment more with their work.

The exhibition examines and distributes his work in 6 rooms according to its features and with the help of experimental technological devices that are inspired by his disruptive attitude towards design.
